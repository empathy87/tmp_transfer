## 2. Watch — high confidence

1. **[Hoozuki no Reitetsu](https://myanimelist.net/anime/20431)** (2014, TV 13 ep; [S2](https://myanimelist.net/anime/35075) + [S2 Part II](https://myanimelist.net/anime/37029) 26 more)
   Deadpan office comedy set in Japanese Hell, where the demon bureaucracy runs on paperwork, HR problems and petty grudges. This is the single closest match in existence to Azazel-san (your 10) and Mairimashita! Iruma-kun (8) — same register of dry supernatural workplace humour, better production. Start here.

2. **[Gintama](https://myanimelist.net/anime/918)** (2006, TV 201 ep + sequels)
   The biggest hole in a 1570-entry list. Samurai-era gag comedy that alternates absurd parody with genuinely sharp action arcs. Normally I'd hesitate to recommend a 367-episode commitment, but you finished Saiki Kusuo (120 ep) at a 9, Beelzebub (60 ep) at a 9, HxH (148 ep) at a 9, and Bakuman (75 ep) at a 9 — you have the tolerance and this is squarely your humour.

3. **[Boku no Kokoro no Yabai Yatsu](https://myanimelist.net/anime/52578)** (2023, TV 12 + 13 ep)
   An edgelord middle-schooler and the tall popular girl who won't leave him alone, slowly becoming unbearably fond of each other. It is the Komi-san (9) / Horimiya (9) lane executed better than either — the best-reviewed romcom of the last five years and absent from your list.

4. **[Ijiranaide, Nagatoro-san](https://myanimelist.net/anime/42361)** (2021, TV 12 + 12 ep)
   Sadistic gyaru relentlessly teases a shy art-club boy; the teasing curdles into real affection. You own Takagi-san, Uzaki-chan, Alya-san, Dosanko Gal and Kubo-san — Nagatoro is the loudest, most Aho Girl-adjacent entry in that whole genre and the one you skipped.

5. **[Kimi ni Todoke 3rd Season](https://myanimelist.net/anime/56538)** (2024, ONA 5 ep)
   You scored S1 **9** and S2 **9**. The Netflix third season finishes the story in five long episodes. Lowest-effort win on this entire page.

6. **[Ouran Koukou Host Club](https://myanimelist.net/anime/853)** (2006, TV 26 ep)
   Scholarship girl mistaken for a boy, conscripted into a host club of rich idiots. Parody-first shoujo — it is structurally Kaichou wa Maid-sama (your 9) five years earlier, and funnier.

7. **[Dr. STONE](https://myanimelist.net/anime/38691)** (2019, TV 24 ep + 3 more seasons)
   Humanity is petrified; a teenage science prodigy rebuilds civilisation from stone-age zero. The "brilliant protagonist teaches and builds, loudly" pleasure of Ansatsu Kyoushitsu (10) and Bakuman (9), with a bright palette and no slow episodes.

8. **[Sket Dance](https://myanimelist.net/anime/9863)** (2011, TV 77 ep)
   School odd-jobs club, one gag per skit, occasional sucker-punch of sincerity. Same Shounen Jump comedy DNA as Gintama and Beelzebub (your 9), lighter commitment.

9. **[Psycho-Pass](https://myanimelist.net/anime/13601)** (2012, TV 22 ep)
   A system predicts criminality before the crime; detectives enforce it, then start doubting it. Your thriller band is unusually consistent — Death Note **9**, Zankyou no Terror **8**, Phantom **8**, Darker than Black **8**, Death Parade **8**, Btooom **8**, Mirai Nikki **8** — and this is the best-built show in it that you've never touched.

10. **[Natsume Yuujinchou](https://myanimelist.net/anime/4081)** (2008, TV 13 ep × 6 seasons + films)
    A boy who can see spirits inherits his grandmother's contract book and spends the series returning names to youkai. **Same author as Hotarubi no Mori e, which you rated 9.** Episodic, gentle, quietly devastating; also lines up with Kamisama Hajimemashita (8) and Fukigen na Mononokean.

11. **[Akatsuki no Yona](https://myanimelist.net/anime/25013)** (2014, TV 24 ep)
    A princess watches her father murdered and her cousin take the throne, then goes looking for four legendary dragon warriors. Akagami no Shirayuki-hime (your 8) with a revenge engine and real fight choreography.

12. **[Sakigake!! Cromartie Koukou](https://myanimelist.net/anime/114)** (2003, 26 shorts)
    Delinquent high school where nobody does anything violent; a gorilla and a robot are enrolled and nobody comments. The direct ancestor of Danshi Koukousei no Nichijou (8) and Sakamoto desu ga? (8). Twelve minutes an episode.

13. **[Osomatsu-san](https://myanimelist.net/anime/31174)** (2015, TV 25 ep)
    Six identical NEET brothers, pure sketch chaos, no continuity. Zero investment required — closest thing on the market to the Azazel-san / Plastic Neesan register.

14. **[Lovely★Complex](https://myanimelist.net/anime/2034)** (2007, TV 24 ep)
    Tall girl, short boy, both loud, both in denial. The most comedy-forward shoujo romcom ever made, which is exactly why it should work on you when quieter shoujo (Tonari no Kaibutsu-kun **6**, Skip to Loafer **6**) didn't.

15. **[Zombie Land Saga](https://myanimelist.net/anime/37976)** (2018, TV 12 ep)
    Dead girls resurrected as an idol group by a screaming lunatic producer. Marketed as an idol show, actually a gag comedy — the joke density is the point.

16. **[Golden Kamuy](https://myanimelist.net/anime/36028)** (2018, TV 12 ep + 3 seasons)
    Russo-Japanese war veteran hunts Ainu gold in Hokkaido with a survival expert. A treasure-hunt thriller that keeps stopping to be extremely stupid about food — the Gokushufudou (8) / Ushio to Tora (8) tonal mix.
